Recommended Model Packs

Pre-configured model stacks with one-click downloads. Pick one that matches your hardware — they include the essential LoRAs for the default Senzu Detail preset.

Quality Notes

Official Flux2 Klein 9B model is gated

The original 18GB BF16 model requires access approval from Black Forest Labs at huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-9B. Our recommended packs use freely available finetuned and quantized versions that don't require gated access.

macOS & AMD Users

FP8 safetensors models don't work on macOS/AMD. Choose the Q8 High Quality or Q4 Low VRAM packs instead. For the best possible quality on these platforms, full-precision GGUF variants are available: BF16 GGUF and F16 GGUF — download them manually via the Custom Model Packs composer below.

Quality in = Quality out

Senzu uses cutting-edge diffusion models. Higher precision models (FP8/BF16) produce noticeably better results than heavily quantized ones. We recommend using the highest quality pack your hardware supports.
